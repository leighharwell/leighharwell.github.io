Computational & Class Projects

  • "Properties and Cosmic Evolution of Compact Object Mergers in LIGO/Virgo Catalogs"
    Homer L. Dodge Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Oklahoma | Spring 2025
    👨‍🏫 Instructor: Dr. Michael Hayden | 🤝 Co-Author: Mason Beaty

    Using gravitational wave evernt catalongs (GWTC-1, GWTC-2, and GWOSC All Events) to create a custom Python pipeline to investigate compact binary merger populations. Computed system chirp masses, gravitational wave energy loss ($\mathcal{M}$ and $E_{\text{GW}}$), and cosmological lookback time to analyze the mass gap distributions, merger rates over the redshift, and heavy element ($r$-process) nucleosynthesis yield.
