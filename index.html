G. Leigh Harwell

Astrophysics Undergraduate | Machine Learning in Asteroseismology & Compact Binary Systems

I am an astrophysics undergraduate researcher at the University of Oklahoma (B.S. Astrophysics, Minor in Mathematics, Expected May 2027) exploring multi-messenger astronomy and compact binary systems. My previous research applied machine learning to spectroscopic data to infer asteroseismic parameters, classify stellar sources, and estimate their ages. My current research leverages multi-messenger observational data to constrain the physical properties of double white dwarf binary systems.

Research Focus

Multi-Messenger Astrophysics & Stellar Systems

Characterizing double white dwarf binary systems using combined photometric and gravitational wave datasets to constrain orbital dynamics, evolution, and candidate properties.

Machine Learning & Stellar Label Estimation

Applying data-driven algorithmic models—such as TheCannon—to asteroseismic and galactic archaeology data to refine automated stellar parameter classification and elemental abundance estimations.
