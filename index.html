G. Leigh Harwell

Astrophysics Undergraduate | Machine Learning in Asteroseismology & Compact Binary Systems

I am an astrophysics undergraduate researcher at the University of Oklahoma (B.S. Astrophysics, Minor in Mathematics, Expected May 2027) exploring stellar astronomy and compact binary systems. My work leverages data-driven algorithmic models and machine learning to analyze multi-messenger observational data, asteroseismology, and galactic archaeology. I am actively seeking graduate research opportunities and Ph.D. programs for Fall 2027.

Research Interests

Multi-Messenger Astrophysics & Stellar Systems

Characterizing double white dwarf binary systems using combined photometric and gravitational wave datasets to constrain orbital dynamics, evolution, and candidate properties.

Machine Learning & Stellar Label Estimation

Applying data-driven algorithmic models—such as TheCannon—to asteroseismic and galactic archaeology data to refine automated stellar parameter classification and elemental abundance estimations.

Research Projects & Experience

  • "Characterization of Double White Dwarf Star Systems"
    Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Oklahoma | August 2026 – Present

    Characterizing double white dwarf binary systems through multi-messenger observational data to constrain system properties and orbital dynamics. Analyzing photometric and gravitational wave datasets to identify compact binary candidates for follow-up observational studies.

  • "Stellar Label Estimation via 'TheCannon' Data-Driven Model"
    NSF Research Experience for Undergraduates (REU) | June 2026 – July 2026

    Implemented TheCannon data-driven algorithmic model to estimate fundamental stellar parameters and elemental abundances from spectroscopic datasets in asteroseismology.

  • "Machine Learning, Asteroseismology, and Galactic Archaeology"
    Department of Physics and Astronomy, University of Oklahoma | October 2024 – October 2026

    Applied machine learning classification algorithms to asteroseismic and galactic archaeology datasets to refine stellar classification models and automated spectroscopic processing pipelines in Python.

  • "Physics for Life Sciences Tutor Coordinator & PLA"
    Student Learning Center, University of Oklahoma | August 2025 – Present

    Coordinate physics tutoring operations for life science majors, developing custom problem sets, conceptual worksheets, and exam review materials tailored to biological applications.
